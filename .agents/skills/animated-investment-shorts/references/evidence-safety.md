# Evidence safety

Source every displayed claim. Preserve its period, population, unit, comparison basis, and reported-versus-calculated status.

Reject or rewrite a claim when:

- a rounded or inferred value looks exact or issuer-reported;
- selected outcomes look representative;
- unlike periods or definitions look directly comparable;
- the visual implies causality the evidence does not establish;
- a derived metric needs more explanation than the insight it provides;
- a restricted fact, image, or logo lacks public-display permission.

Use one short visible qualifier only when its absence would materially reverse the claim. Put methodology and secondary caveats in the report.

Arithmetic shown visually must be valid. Keep fair value separate from target-date market price and distribution percentiles separate from deterministic scenarios.

Record asset provenance and use official logos without distortion.
