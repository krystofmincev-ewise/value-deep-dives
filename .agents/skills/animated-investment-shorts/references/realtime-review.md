# Real-time comparative review

Choose the strongest existing cut as the creative benchmark. Watch the candidate and benchmark uninterrupted at normal speed, first muted and then with one spoken read.

Reject the candidate when it is slower to understand, less visually elegant, less memorable, or harder to retell—even if its facts, bounds, and screenshots pass.

At both 540×960 and 360×640 check:

- the first three seconds;
- one representative moving state and the settled state of each scene;
- every transition once;
- the full autoplay cut without pausing;
- the CTA and disclaimer hold;
- the speaker's actual pace.

Text, paths, glows, tokens, and logo clear space must not collide. Motion should reveal evidence before the conclusion, then settle. Incoming scene copy must remain hidden through the transition.

Use screenshots and deterministic validators to locate defects, not to judge taste. After any material change, rewatch the complete cut rather than expanding a formal approval matrix.
