# AI, software, internet, and semiconductor playbook

## Classify the economics

Distinguish subscription SaaS, consumption cloud, transaction/network, advertising, devices, fabless chips, foundry, equipment, memory, data-center infrastructure, model/API, and services. Mixed businesses usually require SOTP and segment-specific drivers.

## Operating drivers

### Software, cloud, and internet

- ARR or recurring revenue definition, new versus expansion, gross/net retention, bookings, billings, RPO, deferred revenue, seats, usage, price, churn, cohorts, and customer concentration.
- Gross margin after hosting/inference, support, payments, content or traffic-acquisition costs.
- Sales efficiency, CAC payback, incremental margin, free cash flow after SBC, and capitalized development.
- Distribution dependence on app stores, search, social graphs, channels, clouds, or marketplaces.
- Measured product adoption versus demos, sign-ups, benchmark claims, or management anecdotes.

### Semiconductors and infrastructure

- Units, ASP, mix, end demand versus channel inventory, bookings, backlog, lead times, cancellations, utilization, yields, wafer capacity, nodes, packaging, HBM/memory, and equipment intensity.
- Foundry/customer concentration, long-term agreements, supplier prepayments, take-or-pay commitments, and geographic exposure.
- Inventory days, purchase commitments, depreciation lives, capex, working capital, and mid-cycle margins.
- Performance per watt, total cost of ownership, software ecosystem, networking, memory, power/cooling, and deployment constraints.

## Evidence and sources

Prioritize filings, segment notes, customer/supplier filings, product documentation, official benchmarks, export-control text, patents, standards, and reproducible technical experiments. Useful public surfaces include MLCommons, arXiv, USPTO/PatentsView, Census trade, Federal Register/BIS, deps.dev, and documented public benchmark APIs.

Verify the exact benchmark model, dataset, precision, batch, system configuration, submission rules, and sponsor. A benchmark win does not establish availability, customer economics, reliability, or share.

## Accounting and thesis traps

- Treat SBC and dilution consistently; a margin excluding recurring SBC is not owner cash flow.
- Reconcile contracted cloud spend, leases, capex, supplier commitments, and capitalized R&D.
- Separate training demand from inference demand and revenue from ecosystem value transfer.
- Avoid double-counting the same hyperscaler capex across chip, server, networking, power, and software companies.
- Do not infer a public company's proprietary data, distribution, execution, or auction liquidity from a small prototype.
- Distinguish shipment, sell-in, activation, usage, and monetization.
- Export restrictions may affect product eligibility, design cost, inventory, geography, and customer behavior on different dates.

## Valuation and catalysts

Use reverse DCF and unit economics for growth software; EV/revenue or EV/gross-profit only with explicit growth/margin/dilution adjustments. Use mid-cycle earnings/cash flow, capacity, replacement cost, and SOTP for semiconductors and infrastructure.

Catalysts include earnings/guidance, launches, benchmark submissions, large-customer capex, capacity ramps, export rules, standards, design wins, price changes, and product deprecations. Pre-register whether the event can affect shipments, recognized revenue, margins, or only long-term optionality within the forecast horizon.
