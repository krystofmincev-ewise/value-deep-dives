# Substack publishing

Use the user's requested audience, channels, time budget and completion point. Read `DATA_POLICY.md`, `methodology/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`, `methodology/RESEARCH_TOOLING.md`, and the relevant parts of [the publishing workflow](../../../methodology/SUBSTACK_PUBLISHING.md). For a new article, start with [the publication brief](../../../templates/substack-publication-brief.md).

For a narrow edit such as three supplied titles, inspect those articles, change only the requested fields, verify the live results and URLs, and stop. A full audience audit, body rewrite or new distribution package is not required for a narrow edit.

## For a readership audit

- Use the available signed-in Chrome integration and its current runtime documentation. No cookie export, hidden endpoints or new automation runtime is needed.
- Record aggregate baseline metrics before opening public posts. Prefer editor previews: author visits and repeat visits count as views. Store dashboards, backups, screenshots and drafts under ignored `.local/substack-growth/<audit-date>/`.
- Inspect Home, post-level Overview/Reach, Analytics → Sources, and the public About/Notes pages. Record each metric's window and denominator. Check recipients and delivery explicitly; “N/A” opens alone is not proof of web-only publication.
- Do not read subscriber identities or infer which readers are relatives. Source rows can overlap and “Direct” includes unattributed traffic.
- Distinguish insufficient discovery, missing email delivery, weak subscription value, weak article packaging and retention. Tiny samples and unequal article ages cannot identify a winning title or topic.

## Edit for a reader

For a new article or material body rewrite, read [article craft](../../../methodology/ARTICLE_CRAFT.md) and use [the evidence brief](../../../templates/article-evidence-brief.md). Establish what the research adds, trace the central numbers, and outline the causal argument before drafting. Fix a missing research premise in the research; prose polish cannot validate a number. Keep one integrated writer and review the complete result. Preserve the baseline when a candidate loses substance or clarity. Record material corrections and accepted strengths locally, and carry changed evidence into every intended derivative within the authorized scope.

Read the actual article before changing its promise. Name the company, concept or practical problem clearly; use the subtitle to add the payoff. Preserve a good existing hook. A scenario must not become a reported fact in a stronger headline.

When body editing is in scope, improve the opening, repeated explanations and navigation where they obstruct comprehension. Preserve research dates, citations, assumptions, uncertainty, disclosures, figures and calculations. Never invent personal experience or claim human-only authorship. AI detector scores are not editorial objectives; verify current platform policy before asserting any visibility penalty.

Keep a local before-copy for changed fields/body. Distinguish publication description, About, welcome-email template, article title/subtitle, social preview and SEO fields. Avoid broad rebranding without a reason. Retain existing published URLs; a title change does not require changing the slug.

## Upload and publish reliably

- Use normal visible editor controls. For rich text, preserve links, images, captions and subscription components; inspect the rendered result. Do not replace an entire media-rich body just to change one sentence.
- Wait for autosave to show the final title and preview before updating. A textbox can contain new text while the publication preview still shows the previous version.
- Treat draft autosave, “Update now” and email/app delivery as separate states. For an existing-post edit, leave delivery off unless a send is authorized. Never bulk-resend the archive to repair historical delivery omissions.
- Verify the audience, delivery and scheduling controls immediately before publishing. Confirm the live result, unchanged canonical URL and the intended fields. A saved draft alone is not a live update.
- Save a screenshot of the result and maintain a change log, including whether anything was actually sent. Existing author page visits made for QA are not growth.

## Native distribution and measurement

Use useful, self-contained Notes and related-article links when they fit the user's channel preferences. Recommendations should reflect genuine editorial fit; they can notify other authors and readers. Respect existing authorization for publishing, sending, recommending and scheduling; when new outbound communication needs approval, prepare the exact copy and destination first and ask only for the missing authorization. Do not repeat approvals already supplied in the session.

Choose a defined observation age, such as seven days after publication, and report mean, median and individual post results. Separate views, unique visitors, recipients, recipient opens, Note clicks and subscriber conversions. Do not convert lifetime views to seven-day views, assume missing data is zero, subtract guessed family traffic, or promise a readership outcome from same-session edits.

For a growth or publishing-workflow assignment, leave a concrete next publishing package and measurement plan. Add scripts, plugins or recurring tasks only when the actual task warrants them; this skill does not authorize future outbound activity or ongoing monitoring by itself.
