# Snap four-quarter operating forecast

> Draft analyst model. It is not a registered or published forecast.

> **Reader key:** Forecast and valuation shorthand is expanded at first substantive use. See the full [glossary and formula guide](../GLOSSARY.md).

This forecast covers Q3 2026 through Q2 2027. It is an analyst model, not company guidance or sell-side consensus. The purpose is to connect product, advertiser, user, cost, and dilution evidence to observable quarterly outcomes. Dollar figures are millions of United States dollars (USD) unless stated otherwise.

> **Model formulas**
> `Total revenue = advertising revenue + Other Revenue`
> `Year-over-year growth = current-period value / prior-year value - 1`
> `Margin = metric / total revenue`

## Revenue model

The model separates advertising from Other Revenue because their growth and quality differ. The advertising workstream supports a 10–14% next-twelve-month base range, an 18–22% bull range, and 0–5% in a sustained weak-demand case. Other Revenue begins from a much faster 85% Q2 growth rate but should decelerate as the subscriber base grows and the artificial-intelligence (AI) platform partnership anniversary approaches. Other Revenue is not treated as pure subscription revenue because it includes partnership economics.

The base case deliberately avoids 20–30% advertising growth. It assumes approximately 12% ads in each quarter, consistent with gradual auction repair rather than Meta-like parity. Total revenue reaches about 15% because Other Revenue remains above 25% growth. The bull requires advertising yield to turn near-flat or positive while impressions continue to grow; it is not simply “better AI.”

Q3 Other Revenue now follows a monotonic scenario order. It is not a residual plug used to force total revenue toward guidance: the bear falls below management's range, the base sits near its midpoint, and the bull clears the high end.

## Capital-allocation and dilution bridge

The model does not assume that all headline FCF accumulates as cash or that buybacks create value independently. It uses the following explicit one-year bridge from Q2 2026 through Q2 2027, the latest likely reported quarter at the August target date. “Cash not retained” is a residual covering repurchases, debt/other financing actions, and changes in financial assets outside the simplified net-debt perimeter; restructuring cash is already reflected in operating cash flow and is not deducted again. The residual is not added elsewhere in valuation.

## Operating mechanisms behind the base case

1. **Advertising demand:** Conversions API (CAPI), Smart tools, Dynamic Product Ads (DPA), app optimization, Sponsored Snaps, and Promoted Places support low-teens advertiser spend. Effective cost per thousand impressions (eCPM) improves from the Q1 2026 decline but is not assumed to become strongly positive immediately.
2. **Subscriptions and partnerships:** the paying community grows beyond 25 million and Memories/Lens+/Platinum add revenue, but growth decelerates. No software-as-a-service (SaaS) multiple or undisclosed churn advantage is embedded.
3. **High-value users:** North America DAU remains around 90–92 million and Europe broadly stable. Average revenue per user (ARPU) continues to offset slight user pressure. Rest-of-World DAU grows but receives limited ARPU-convergence credit.
4. **Costs:** personnel savings show from Q3, while AI infrastructure and Specs remain inside management's disclosed 2026 envelope. The model does not assume all $500 million of annualized savings becomes FCF.
5. **Cash quality:** FCF is held below a simple 75% EBITDA conversion rule because working capital, interest, cloud commitments, and product investment are volatile. Expected share growth is modeled explicitly rather than treating buybacks as value creation.

## What the model does not know

- No dated, reproducible sell-side consensus set is used, so this model forecasts results rather than “earnings surprises.”
- Snap does not disclose subscription churn, plan mix, gross margin, subscriber geography, or how much Other Revenue comes from the AI-platform partner.
- It does not disclose ad revenue, eCPM, impressions, or contribution profit by product surface or full geography each quarter.
- Specs cost and unit economics are not separately reported.
- Legal outcomes and product remedies have fat-tailed effects that do not fit a smooth quarterly model.

## Model conclusion

The highest-confidence near-term improvement is cost leverage, because management has removed personnel and guides the cost base. The highest-importance uncertainty is advertising yield, because more inventory has repeatedly coincided with lower eCPM. The base case supports a healthier business but not an automatic stock rerating: the valuation still depends on whether the market begins to credit headline FCF despite SBC, dilution, founder control, and hardware/regulatory risk.
