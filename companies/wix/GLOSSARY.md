# Wix glossary

- **ARR:** annual recurring revenue at the measurement date. It is not identical to recognized revenue or cash.
- **Base44:** Wix's prompt-to-application platform, acquired in June 2025. It is not `n8n`.
- **Bookings:** contractual value of subscriptions and business solutions booked during a period, adjusted for Wix's stated definitions.
- **Business Solutions:** payments, commerce, bookings, marketing, CRM and other tools layered on Wix sites.
- **Creative Subscriptions:** Wix's website/application creation and hosting subscription products; Wix reports Base44 here.
- **Harmony:** Wix's January 2026 flagship AI website builder, combining conversational generation with direct visual editing.
- **MCP:** Model Context Protocol, an open interface that lets AI agents discover and invoke software tools. Wix exposes creation and business-management functions through MCP.
- **Owner free cash flow:** cash left for owners after economically recognizing dilution. This dossier uses raw FCF and an explicit SBC/dilution adjustment, not management's adjusted FCF alone.
- **Partner:** an agency, freelancer or other professional building sites for customers on Wix, mainly through Wix Studio.
- **RPO:** remaining performance obligations—contracted revenue not yet recognized.
- **SOTP:** sum-of-the-parts valuation.
- **Vibe coding:** creating software or sites primarily through natural-language prompts to an AI model.
