# Wix research plan

## Decision question

At $87.62, does Wix offer sufficient six- and twelve-month fair-value upside to justify adding to an existing winning position, after separating installed-base durability from AI exposure in new customer acquisition?

## Workstreams

1. Reconcile FY2021–Q2 2026 revenue, bookings, ARR, margins, cash flow, SBC, debt, shares and guidance from filings.
2. Audit buybacks and the 2026 debt-funded tender rather than treating them automatically as insider confidence.
3. Map the three product layers: legacy Editor/Studio, Harmony, and Base44.
4. Review the product from public professional reviews and user anecdotes, keeping selection bias explicit.
5. Inventory high-signal founder/executive interviews, especially 20VC, and distinguish transcript-checked passages from show-note claims.
6. Use licensed aggregate workforce data for composition, geography, tenure proxies and talent flows; keep captures local-only.
7. Compare Lovable, Replit and incumbent builders; correct private valuation and tax claims.
8. Assess OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Kimi and compute scaling over six to twelve months.
9. Triangulate SOTP, owner-FCF multiple and DCF; freeze a joint-horizon distribution contract.
10. State action, falsifiers, evidence gaps and next review independent of purchase price.

## Known limits

- Wix does not disclose cohort gross retention, Base44 audited standalone revenue/margin, MCP adoption, or churn by product.
- Private-company ARR and valuation claims are unaudited and not directly comparable with public equity value.
- Twenty-three complete visible YouTube transcripts across Wix, Base44, Lovable and Replit were reviewed sequentially and reduced to timestamped analysis. The temporary-export process estimated roughly 239,000 words; because the exports were deleted by design, that is an order-of-magnitude workflow measure rather than a reproducible evidence count. Auto-caption errors and interview incentives remain limitations. One additional Lovable/Stripe video lacked a transcript and is catalogued only. The 20Growth CMO episode appears to be audio-only on its public distribution surfaces and remains show-note evidence.
- Gemini's dedicated Deep Research control was unavailable in the current signed-in UI. A standard Pro response was used only for source discovery, not as evidence; inaccurate suggestions were rejected and no output was retained.
- The valuation distribution is structured elicitation in shadow-model status, not an empirically calibrated probability forecast.
