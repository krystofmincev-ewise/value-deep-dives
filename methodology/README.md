# Methodology

These documents define how research is created, versioned, evaluated, and disclosed.

- [Investment process](INVESTMENT_PROCESS.md): the funnel from discovery to retrospective
- [Research standards](RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md): evidence, sourcing, LLM use, and publication rules
- [Research tooling](RESEARCH_TOOLING.md): APIs, browsers, authenticated sources, and credential boundaries
- [Biopharma evidence research](BIOPHARMA_RESEARCH.md): papers, trials, targets, regulatory evidence, and probability-of-success workflow
- [Research capability architecture](CAPABILITY_ARCHITECTURE.md): canonical entities, deterministic/agent boundaries, and capability roadmap
- [Event-driven research](EVENT_DRIVEN_RESEARCH.md): catalyst pre-registration, leakage controls, outcome resolution, and calibration
- [Open-source tooling review](OPEN_SOURCE_TOOLING_REVIEW.md): dated build-versus-integrate assessment of public repositories
- [Valuation framework](VALUATION_FRAMEWORK.md): scenarios, target prices, and comparability
- [Performance and disclosures](PERFORMANCE_AND_DISCLOSURES.md): prospective scoring and position disclosures
- [Report presentation plan](REPORT_PRESENTATION_PLAN.md): GitHub-native generated reports, analytical visualizations, and delivery roadmap
- [Post-Snap research and valuation audit](2026-W34-POST-SNAP-RESEARCH-AUDIT.md): three-angle review, immediate corrections, remaining limitations, and harness roadmap

Methodology changes are allowed, but they should be dated, explained, and applied prospectively. A new rule must not be used to rewrite an old forecast's evaluation after its outcome is known.
