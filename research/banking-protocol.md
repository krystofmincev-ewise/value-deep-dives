# Optional bank experiment: protocol before results

Requested 11 October 2026, with a two-to-three-hour research/implementation budget.
The outcome is a separate reproducible experiment and an optional final section
of both reading editions. Existing no-bank results remain authoritative for the
main story. This is a new fictional extension, not calibration to a real bank.

## Question and discriminating cases

Can fair, finite credit change an interruption into a recoverable event? Can it
sustain consumption when income permanently disappears? Can financing alone
remove a physical production constraint?

1. Matched disabled-credit control versus a payroll bridge during a temporary
   equipment-order freeze. A success must include all promised wages paid AND
   the loan actually repaid from later receipts, without refinancing the payment.
2. A temporary household-income interruption followed by recovery, compared with
   permanent income loss. Subsequent consumption reductions used to repay must
   be reported, not hidden by a favorable short horizon.
3. A known permanent income loss for which a prudent bank refuses unsecured
   credit; separately, an initially credible recovery forecast that proves wrong.
   Lending into known permanent zero income is not called ordinary prudent banking.
4. A financing-constrained investment case if it can be implemented cleanly;
   compare spare versus binding real capacity and distinguish replacement from
   net additions to equipment.

## Accounting and comparison contract

One bank hosts all deposits. The inherited base cash is200. Capitalize the bank
with20 from the owners’ existing firm cash buffers, before period0 and without
calling it operating income: workers60, owners40, firms80 of deposits. The bank
holds the inherited settlement asset200 against deposits180 and equity20.
Its owners exchange part of their firm equity for bank equity; no wealth gift is
created. Both enabled- and disabled-credit arms use exactly this opening book.
This is an explicit reset from the earlier town’s firm buffers100/deposits200.

At every event: settlement assets + loans = deposits + bank equity.
Origination adds a loan and matching deposit. Principal repayment removes both.
Interest received reduces deposits and raises bank income/equity until paid out.
Bank dividends contain realized profit, never principal repayments. Defaults
reduce assets/equity, not deposits already held by somebody else. Distinguish an
accounting write-off from debt forgiveness and document the chosen resolution.
New loans cannot repay their own opening-period debt service by construction.

Bank fairness is a declared rule, not a claim to solve optimal banking: no fees,
penalty prices or deliberate exploitation; finite borrower credit limits, explicit
income expectations and a finite capital constraint. Zero interest is a control;
a modest invented per-round rate is a sensitivity. Model rounds are not years.
No separately produced banking services, operating costs, withdrawals, competing
banks, deposit insurance, government bailout or central-bank policy response are
modeled. The inherited settlement asset is fixed, not an outside rescue flow.

## Acceptance evidence

- Transaction tests cover creation, transfers, repayment, interest, distribution,
  refusal, partial service and loss resolution; invalid/non-finite inputs rejected.
- Per-period loan, deposit and equity changes reconcile to event flows, and
  production value added reconciles to final purchases independently of finance.
- Disabled lending reproduces the existing cash model with matching initial firm
  buffers80. Existing original outputs and tests remain unchanged.
- Matched controls and sensitivity runs expose debt service, credit refusal,
  arrears, defaults, bank capital and bank insolvency as distinct events.
- Narrative claims refer to actual saved time-stamped rows, with clear scenario
  resets. New diagrams are static first, then shared with the animated edition.
- Targeted independent accounting/economics review precedes final wording.

No predetermined verdict is required. Unexpected failures, including a bridge
that cannot be repaid after orders return, are useful results to preserve.
