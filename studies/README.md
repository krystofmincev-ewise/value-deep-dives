# Cross-company studies

Studies explain a research question that spans companies or requires its own
reproducible experiment. Each study keeps its article, evidence, model, limitations
and publication record together.

- [Who buys what AI makes?](toy-economy/README.md): conditional toy economies about
  wages, automation, prices, spending, ownership and finance. Start with the combined
  article; earlier papers and simulation outputs remain available for reproduction.

Company-specific evidence and valuations belong in [company dossiers](../companies/README.md).
