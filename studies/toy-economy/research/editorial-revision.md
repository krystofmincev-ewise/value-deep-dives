  that one firm's payroll is another firm's customer before explaining the model.
- Introduce only the accounts needed to follow the story. Then trace the private
  saving into three spending outcomes: continued circulation, a smaller economy,
  or sales and investment declining together.
- Follow those possible endings with the transition problem: bills can fall due
  before another source of spending arrives. Distinguish a stopped payroll path
  from output approaching zero and from survival at lower output.
- Ask how much output could ultimately be produced, then show why maximizing
  gross GDP does not maximize household consumption.
- Return to the investor's missing customer assumptions and finish with the
  original person's ability to afford the car. The final point concerns income
  reaching that person, rather than automation being inherently good or bad.
