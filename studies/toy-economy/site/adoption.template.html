Who is working—and getting paid?

The shop cards show actual results for the selected round. A full shift is one person's normal work time per round. Contracted time and time actually needed for orders can differ. Headcount alone cannot tell you the wage bill.

Inspect each shop's adoption prediction and choice

A shop that chooses whether to adopt compares two one-step predictions before making its choice. These are predictions, not actual results or forecasts of the complete path. Operating surplus means sales minus wages and AI fees, before new equipment and depreciation. A scheduled path simply sets each shop's automation stage.
