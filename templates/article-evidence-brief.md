# Article evidence brief

Copy into ignored `.local/` for a new article or material rewrite. Keep it as
short as the work allows; link the existing research rather than reproducing it.
Use [Article craft](../methodology/ARTICLE_CRAFT.md). A narrow correction does not
require a new full brief. The separate
[publication brief](substack-publication-brief.md) controls packaging and delivery.

## Argument in order

Write a short outline in connected steps. For each section, state the reader's
question, the evidence that answers it, the resulting inference and the question
that follows. Include the strongest countercase where it matters.
